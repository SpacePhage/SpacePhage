## 🌠 About Me

My name is Ryan Mayberry and I am a front-end web developer with a passion for astronomy, science communication, and digital design.

I currently work at **Lowell Observatory** as a Telescope Supervisor and Maintenance Technician, where I help create engaging educational experiences and introduce visitors to the wonders of the night sky.

My background in astronomy outreach has inspired me to combine my knowledge with my web development skills to make scientific information more accessible, engaging, and interactive.

I'm particularly interested in developing responsive websites and educational tools that connect people with astronomy and recent scientific discoveries.
